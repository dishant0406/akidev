**Full description**

Akidev is a reader. It has no content of its own: you add the sources and books you already use, and it gives you one calm place to read them.

Manga and comics
- Follow series from sources you install, with a long strip, paged or right-to-left reading
- Library with categories, history, new chapter alerts and downloads for offline reading
- Open your own CBZ files

Books
- Open EPUB files from your phone or add a catalogue (OPDS) by its address
- Pages or scrolling, bundled reading fonts, themes, warm tint and brightness
- Highlights, notes, search, contents and where you stopped
- Read aloud: a voice that runs on your phone, with the sentence marked as you listen

Private by design
- No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
- Everything you read and save stays on your phone; back it up to a file when you want to move it

Akidev does not include, offer or endorse any source or catalogue. Add only what you have the right to read.
